dag-failure-agent — benchmark & weight tuning

Every score below is recomputed in-page from each run's raw signals using the same weighted-mean formula as processor/agent/confidence.py. Move a slider and the decisions move with it.

Weight playground

Positive weights are a weighted mean over the signals present on a run; the penalty is subtracted afterwards. Signals a run doesn't have are dropped and the mean renormalised — never guessed.
